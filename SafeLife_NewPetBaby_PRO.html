Evidence base: CDC and WHO guidance on toxoplasmosis and zoonotic disease prevention · AVMA guidance on pet-child household safety · recognised paediatric and veterinary behaviour guidance on infant-pet supervision
This checklist is educational general safety guidance for welcoming a baby into a home with pets. Pregnant household members and anyone who is immunocompromised should discuss toxoplasmosis and other zoonotic disease risk specifically with their own healthcare provider. For pet behaviour concerns, consult your veterinarian or a qualified animal behaviourist for advice specific to your household.
